## We've Got You Covered

Clear benefits, real support

At GitLab, we believe team member benefits should be simple to understand and there when you need them. From life's big moments to everyday questions, our comprehensive coverage gives you clarity and confidence so you can focus on your work and life.

## Health & Wellbeing

### Private Medical Insurance

GitLab provides comprehensive private medical coverage through VHI, giving you access to quality medical treatments, health screenings, and protection against high medical costs. We cover 100% of the premiums for you and your eligible dependents.

 Eligibility Requirements

- All full-time team members
- Coverage includes:

  - Team member's spouse
  - Domestic partner
  - Children up to age 21 (if enrolled in school). Please make sure to certify that your dependent is still enrolled in school when enrolling them. To do so, members need to sign up using the Online Enrolment portal and there will be a prompt to confirm Yes or No if they are a Student, there is no further details provided at this point as VHI will accept the members answer upon application.

 Coverage & Key Features

- Access to private medical care and treatments
- Comprehensive health screenings
- 100% premium coverage by GitLab
- Plan details (PMI 4316)
- Benefits brochure
- 2025 Renewal Webinar Recording

 Lifetime Community Rating (LCR)

From 1st of May 2015, if you are 35 or over when you first take out private health insurance, you will pay an additional amount of 2% for each year that you are aged 35 and over. Please contact VHI if you think this may apply.

 How to Enroll

1. Visit the VHI online portal or call VHI
2. Enter your GitLab Employee ID as "Employee Number"
3. Enter your GitLab Department (from Workday) as "Cost Centre"
4. Choose PMI 4316 or call 056 777 5800 to discuss your needs

 Important Dates

- Annual renewal: November 1
- New hires can enroll from hire date
- Policy auto-renews annually

 Contact Information

- VHI Corporate Advice Line: 056 777 5800
- Hospital Directory

 Tax Considerations

Health insurance is treated as a Benefit in Kind under Revenue Commissioner rules. Your normal tax rate applies. Learn more about tax implications.

### Eye Care

Under the Safety, Health, and Welfare at Work Regulations 2007, GitLab provides eye care benefits for team members.

 Coverage & Key Features

- Regular eye and eyesight exam reimbursement
- Up to €140 reimbursement for computer glasses (if medically required for VDU use only)
- Submit VHI claims first, then remaining costs through Navan

 How to Claim

1. Get eye exam and prescription
2. Submit eligible costs through VHI first
3. Submit remaining receipts via Navan and email the prescription to total-rewards@gitlab.com

## Financial Security

### Pension Plan

GitLab offers a defined contribution pension scheme through Aviva, administered by NFP Ireland, designed to help you build financial security for retirement. Effective 2025-11-01, all new hires will be automatically enrolled under our company plan.

  NEW (January 2026) - Pension Auto Enrolment Regulation

- Starting January 1, 2026, Ireland's new Auto Enrolment pension scheme (called "My Future Fund") becomes mandatory. All eligible employees who are not already contributing to a workplace pension scheme will be automatically enrolled in this government-run pension program.
- **Opportunity**: Team Members can join our superior company DC pension plan and avoid being automatically enrolled in the less beneficial government scheme.
- **Key Advantages of our Company Plan**: 3-5% employer matching contribution (compared to 1.5% employer contribution in Auto Enrolment's first phase), complete contribution flexibility, income tax relief, flexible investment options and no mandatory waiting periods.
- See the Ireland Auto Enrolment Pension Transition FAQ for detailed information

  GitLab Pension Scheme Coverage

- New hires are automatically enrolled under the Aviva plan, with a 3% Employee and Employer contribution
- Team members can increase their contribution and GitLab matches up to 5% of annual base salary
- Option for team members to make Additional Voluntary Contributions (AVCs)
- Professional administration through NFP Ireland
- Transfers accepted from other pension plans
- See the Pension information video

 How to Enroll or Change Your Contributions

1. Complete and submit the Aviva Pension Form with your details

 Important Details & Resources

- Enrollments after the 7th process the following month
- Review investments through Aviva's Fund Centre
- New joiner FAQ
- Leaver options guide

 Contact Details

- Email: employeebenefits@nfpireland.ie

### Income Protection (Disability Coverage)

Full-time team members receive disability coverage providing 66% of base salary (less social welfare) to age 65.

Ireland's statutory sick leave policy offers a floor level of protection and does not interfere with existing, more favorable, sick pay schemes such as GitLab's Sick Time policy. When allowed by law, GitLab's Sick Leave policy will run concurrently with this leave policy.

- 66% of base salary coverage
- 13-week deferred period
- Coverage to age 65
- Claim form
- Early intervention services

 How to File a Claim

GitLab will reach out to you within your first month of absence to initiate the Income Application Process, should your absence be extended and go beyond 13+ weeks.

## Growing Family Leaves

### Maternity Leave

GitLab provides comprehensive maternity leave support that goes beyond statutory requirements.

 Statutory Entitlement

- 26 weeks ordinary maternity leave
- Additional 16 weeks unpaid leave available
- Must start 2 weeks before due date
- Minimum 4 weeks after birth

 GitLab Support

- 100% salary for up to 16 weeks (includes state benefit top-up)
- State Maternity Benefit: €262/week (subject to PRSI)
- Support throughout your leave journey

 How to Apply

1. Notify leaves@gitlab.com with start/end dates
2. Receive completed MB2 form from Absence Management
3. Apply via gov.ie at least 6 weeks before leave
4. Submit time off in Workday (Parental Leave category)

### Adoptive Leave

Comprehensive support for growing families.

 Statutory Entitlement

- 24 weeks ordinary adoptive leave
- Additional 16 weeks unpaid leave available
- Begins on placement date

 GitLab Support

- 100% salary for up to 16 weeks (includes state benefit top-up)
- State Adoptive Benefit: €262/week (subject to PRSI)

 How to Apply

1. Notify leaves@gitlab.com with dates
2. Apply via gov.ie at least 6 weeks before leave
3. Submit time off in Workday

### Paternity Leave

Time to bond with your newborn.

 Eligibility

- Father of the child
- Partner of the mother
- Parent of donor-conceived child

 Leave Entitlement

- 2 consecutive weeks within first 6 months
- State Paternity Benefit: €262/week (subject to PRSI)
- GitLab tops up to 100% salary

 How to Apply

1. Notify leaves@gitlab.com with due date
2. Receive PB2 form from Absence Management
3. Apply via gov.ie at least 4 weeks before leave

### Parent's Leave

Extended support for new parents.

 Leave Entitlement

- 9 weeks leave in child's first 2 years
- Can be taken consecutively or separately
- State Parent's Benefit available (subject to PRSI)
- GitLab salary top-up applies if taken within the first 16 weeks of GitLab's Parental Leave

 How to Apply

1. Notify leaves@gitlab.com 6 weeks in advance
2. Apply for benefit via gov.ie 4 weeks before leave

### Parental Leave

Flexibility for ongoing childcare needs.

 Leave Entitlement

- Up to 26 weeks unpaid leave
- For children under 12 (16 with disability)
- Can split into blocks with manager approval
- Requires 1 year employment

 How to Apply

1. Notify leaves@gitlab.com 6 weeks in advance
2. Sign confirmation document 4 weeks before leave

### Midwife Support (for VHI Members)

VHI's Midwife Support Service provides expectant parents with dedicated support throughout pregnancy and after birth.

 Key Features

- Three antenatal consultations (one per trimester)
- Four postnatal consultations in first six weeks
- Personal guidance through your pregnancy journey
- Education and support at each stage

 How to Access

- Register via VHI App or email midwife@vhi.ie
- Receive confirmation and call within 2 working days
- Service details

### Carer's Leave

Support when caring for loved ones.

 Leave Entitlement

- Up to 104 weeks unpaid leave
- For full-time care of qualifying person
- Minimum 13-week periods
- GitLab provides 100% salary for first 25 working days if receiving state benefit

 How to Apply

1. Submit written notice to leaves@gitlab.com 6 weeks in advance
2. Provide Department decision on care requirements
3. Complete confirmation document 2 weeks before leave
